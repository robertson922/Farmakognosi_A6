⚔️ The Gauntlet

10 random structures, one at a time. Beat the clock.

  • ⏱️ 3 minutes for each structure.
  • 🚀 Time bonus: your points are worth 200% at the start and fall smoothly, every second, to 50% when the 3 minutes are up.
  • 🎯 1 point for every atom you assign correctly (optional atoms are worth nothing).
  • ✅ You only score if the whole structure is correct.
  • 🌿 Terpene bonus: terpene atoms count double if you also put them into units of exactly 5 touching atoms. Any valid split counts.
  • ⚠️ A wrong "Check my answer" costs 15 seconds.
  • 🙈 No answers are shown. You can skip a structure for 0 points.
  • ⚖️ Every set has roughly the same number of atoms, so it's fair.
